Space Crops and Bioregenerative Agriculture

Future crews will need crops that produce nutrition reliably under constrained water, light, volume, gravity, and labor conditions.

Murphy Knowledge Project • Reviewed September 2026

Growing food in space is not simply ordinary farming moved indoors. A space crop system must control water, oxygen at the root zone, lighting, microbes, temperature, nutrients, and food safety while using as little mass, volume, power, and crew time as possible.

What NASA is already testing

The International Space Station hosts plant-growth systems including Veggie and the Advanced Plant Habitat. The Advanced Plant Habitat is an enclosed, automated growth chamber with environmental controls and extensive sensing, allowing researchers to study crop development under spaceflight conditions.

NutritionFresh crops can supplement stored food with nutrients and dietary variety during long missions.
Life supportPlants can contribute oxygen generation, carbon-dioxide uptake, transpiration, and water cycling.
Human factorsLiving plants may also support crew well-being in isolated, confined habitats.

The hard problems

Space crop research still has major gaps: delivering both water and oxygen to roots, understanding radiation effects, controlling microbes and pathogens, maintaining viable seed stocks, selecting cultivars, recycling nutrients, and determining how partial gravity changes plant development.

From “salad machine” to food system

A settlement cannot depend only on lettuce. A mature food system needs a portfolio: fast-growing leafy greens, calorie crops, protein sources, herbs, fruits, and crops selected for harvest index, reliability, processing needs, storage, and waste recovery. Crop residues themselves become part of the habitat's material cycle.

ALCHEMY design question

The key question is not “Can plants grow on Mars?” It is “What crop mix, growth system, power budget, water loop, and nutrient loop can repeatedly feed a population while recovering from failures?” That systems-level question is where agriculture and life support merge.
