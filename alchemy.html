FEATURED SYSTEMS PROJECT

Project ALCHEMY

Designing a progressively self-reliant human settlement by connecting life support, food, energy, local resources, manufacturing, medicine, and habitat engineering into one system.

THE CENTRAL QUESTION

What would it take to make a distant settlement truly livable?

ALCHEMY treats settlement as a systems-engineering problem. No single technology makes a colony sustainable. The settlement survives only when its major loops support one another, contain redundancy, and can be repaired with the resources available.

ALCHEMY principle: close the loops

Every waste stream should be examined as a potential resource. Every critical function should have a backup path. Every imported consumable should be treated as a target for eventual local production or recycling.
