Frontier Ideas & Thought Experiments

What might humanity build next—and which parts are science, experimental technology, theoretical engineering, or informed speculation? Explore ambitious ideas without confusing the possible with the proven.

HOW MKP LABELS CLAIMS

Curiosity with clear boundaries

These categories describe the specific claim under discussion, not an entire field. An idea can contain demonstrated components while its overall proposal remains speculative.

01

Demonstrated

Observed, built, measured, or repeatedly tested for the claim being made. Real-world constraints and limitations still matter.

02

Experimental

Being investigated or tested, but its wider application, performance, or feasibility is not yet established.

03

Theoretical

Supported by mathematical models or established physical principles, without a full-scale demonstrated implementation.

04

Speculative

A possible scenario or thought experiment that depends on major unanswered engineering, scientific, economic, or social questions.

Ideas to investigate

SPACE

Rotating habitats and megastructures

Status: theoretical / speculative. Rotational artificial gravity follows established mechanics, but large self-sustaining orbital cities and Dyson-swarm-scale infrastructure have not been built. What would the mass, energy, shielding and maintenance budgets look like?

Explore MKP's settlement framework →
PLANETS

Terraforming and planetary engineering

Status: speculative. Changing a planet's global climate or atmosphere would involve enormous resources and uncertain biological and ecological consequences. Compare possible approaches with nearer-term, enclosed habitats.

Explore Project ALCHEMY →
TRAVEL

Interstellar voyages and generation ships

Status: theoretical / speculative. Known physics can frame travel-time and propulsion limits, but multigenerational spacecraft demand unresolved solutions for closed-loop ecology, reliability, governance, and health.

Start with life-support evidence →
ENERGY

Fusion and future energy systems

Status: demonstrated physics; experimental power engineering. Fusion reactions and ignition experiments are real, but economical, reliable fusion electricity at grid scale remains a development challenge. Avoid treating breakthroughs as operating commercial plants.

Read MKP's fusion overview →
AI

Advanced AI, superintelligence and long-term futures

Status: actively researched with speculative future scenarios. Contemporary AI capabilities are measurable, while predictions about future systems, timelines, social change and existential outcomes are uncertain and contested.

Explore MKP's AI section →
LIFE

Alien life and the Fermi paradox

Status: scientific search plus speculation. Astronomical observations and planetary chemistry inform the search for life; no confirmed extraterrestrial civilization has been established. Ask what evidence would change our conclusions.

Discuss possibilities without presenting guesses as facts

Questions for readers: Which proposed technology has the strongest supporting evidence? Which unknown is the biggest obstacle? What experiment or calculation could test the idea? What unintended consequences should be investigated?

MKP welcomes respectful discussion and challenges to its conclusions. Use original wording and cite your sources. Please link to outside videos and articles rather than uploading copyrighted footage, screenshots, illustrations, transcripts, or large passages without permission or a sound legal basis.
