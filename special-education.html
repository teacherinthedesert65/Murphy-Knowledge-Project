PRACTICE LIBRARY

Special Education

Connecting evidence-informed practice with the daily work of helping students access instruction, build skills, and become more independent.

IN

Interventions

Targeted, explicit instruction built from student data: model the skill, provide guided practice, give timely feedback, and adjust intensity when progress stalls.

IG

IEP Goals

Useful goals identify a meaningful skill, establish a measurable condition and criterion, and make it possible for the team to determine whether instruction is working.

PM

Progress Monitoring

Frequent, consistent measures help teams compare student performance over time, recognize trends, and make instructional decisions instead of waiting for an annual review.

AT

Assistive Technology

Tools should reduce barriers without lowering the learning target: text-to-speech, speech-to-text, alternative input, visual supports, communication systems, and other access tools can increase participation and independence.

CS

Classroom Strategies

Clear routines, chunked directions, visual supports, opportunities to respond, explicit modeling, structured choices, reinforcement, and predictable feedback can make instruction more accessible.

AM

Accommodations & Modifications

Accommodations change how a student accesses or demonstrates learning; modifications change what the student is expected to learn. Teams should select supports based on individual need rather than disability label alone.

This library is designed as a growing bridge between research and classroom practice. Future articles will expand each topic with examples, implementation guidance, and source references.
