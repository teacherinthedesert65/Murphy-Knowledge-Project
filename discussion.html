MKP Discussion

A place to question claims, compare evidence, suggest sources and help identify the next Murphy Knowledge Project deep dive.

The rule is simple

Challenge ideas, not people. Strong disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, spam and deliberate misinformation are not. When making a factual claim, bring the best source you can find—and be willing to change your mind when stronger evidence appears.

⌕

Share papers, archaeological reports, primary sources or credible reporting that supports—or challenges—an MKP conclusion.

✦

Found an unusual scientific, historical or technological claim? Propose it for an evidence-based investigation.

↻

Revisit a Conclusion

MKP conclusions are not sacred. New evidence can change them, and the reasoning should be visible.

Discussion standards

Separate observation from interpretation. Identify speculation as speculation. Prefer primary sources when they are available. Avoid posting private personal information. Do not copy large portions of copyrighted material; link to the source and summarize the relevant evidence instead. Claims involving medicine, archaeology, history or science should be evaluated according to the quality of evidence rather than how exciting the claim is.
