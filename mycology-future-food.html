FUTURE AGRICULTURE · CROSSOVER TOPIC

Fungi, Mycology & the Future of Food

Fungi are more than mushrooms: they connect food production, ecosystems, biotechnology, manufacturing, medicine, recycling, and the challenge of sustaining people beyond Earth.

FO

Edible Fungi & Nutrition

Explore edible mushrooms, fungal proteins, mycoprotein, nutrition, flavor, texture, and the possibilities and limits of fungi as future food sources.

SO

Soil, Plants & Ecosystems

Fungi decompose organic matter, cycle nutrients, and form important relationships with plants. Future agriculture depends on understanding these living networks rather than treating soil as an inert growing medium.

RE

Waste into Resources

Some fungal systems can help transform organic residues into useful biomass or materials. This connects food production with circular systems in which waste becomes an input for another process.

HOW TO READ FUTURE SCIENCE

Evidence before excitement

Throughout MKP, emerging ideas should be understood by their evidence level: Established Science for well-supported knowledge and demonstrated methods; Active Research for work currently being tested; Plausible Future Application for ideas grounded in known science but not yet demonstrated at the proposed scale or setting; and Speculation for possibilities that remain substantially untested. The goal is to explore boldly without confusing possibility with proof.
