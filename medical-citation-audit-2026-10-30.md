## Verification standards

- Prefer primary peer-reviewed publications and trial registries; label sponsor and institutional announcements.
- Distinguish cell, animal, observational, randomized human, and regulatory evidence.
- Verify DOI, authors, study design, sample sizes, endpoints, absolute effects, harms, and limitations before endorsing a claim.
- Do not treat a trial endpoint press release as proof of overall survival, regulatory approval, or independently reviewed results.
