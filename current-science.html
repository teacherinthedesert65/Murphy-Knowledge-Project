How Current Science works

This page is intentionally selective. New findings will be added when they are interesting, well sourced, and useful to the larger Murphy Knowledge Project. Early results will be identified as early results, animal studies will not be presented as human treatments, and intriguing chemistry will not be called evidence of life unless the evidence actually supports that conclusion.
