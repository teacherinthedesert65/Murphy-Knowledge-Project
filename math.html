TRY THE IDEA

Problems first. Vocabulary second.

Can you double a recipe without changing the result? Can data tell whether a cold-adapted crop really performs better? Can measurements help reconstruct an event? Can you estimate the resources needed for a space mission? Each question gives the mathematics a reason to exist before introducing formal terminology.
